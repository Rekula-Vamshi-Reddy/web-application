Chicken Biryani

A classic Indian rice dish loved by millions

Chicken Biryani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, tender chicken, and a blend of spices. This recipe serves 4-6 people and takes approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes to prepare and cook.

Ingredients

For the Rice

2 cups Basmati Rice
4 cups Water
2 Bay Leaves
4 Cardamom Pods
4 Cloves
1 Cinnamon Stick Salt to taste

For Chicken Masala

500g Chicken (cut into pieces)
2 Onions (sliced thin)
2 Tomatoes (chopped)
1 cup Yogurt
2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
2 Green Chilies
1 tsp Turmeric Powder
2 tsp Red Chili Powder
1 tsp Garam Masala
Fresh Mint and Coriander Leaves
3 tbsp Oil or Ghee
Salt to taste

For Garnishing

Fried Onions (Birista)
Fresh Mint Leaves
Saffron soaked in warm milk
1 tbsp Ghee

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Rice

Wash the basmati rice 3-4 times until the water runs clear. Soak in water for 30 minutes, then drain. Boil 4 cups of water with bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon. Add rice and cook until 70% done. Drain and set aside.

Step 2: Cook the Chicken

Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Add sliced onions and fry until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, saute for 2 minutes. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.

Step 3: Add Spices

Add turmeric, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well and cook for 2 minutes. Add chicken pieces and cook on high heat for 5 minutes. Add yogurt and mix well. Cover and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes until chicken is tender.

Step 4: Layer the Biryani

In a large heavy pot, spread the chicken masala at the bottom. Layer the partially cooked rice on top. Sprinkle fried onions, mint leaves, coriander leaves, and saffron milk on top. Add a tablespoon of ghee. Cover tightly with a lid.

Step 5: Dum Cooking (Steam)

Cook on high heat for 3-4 minutes, then reduce to the lowest heat and cook for 25-30 minutes. Do not open the lid during this process. Turn off heat and let it rest for 5 minutes.

Step 6: Serve

Gently mix the layers with a fork. Serve hot with raita, salan, or a simple onion salad. Garnish with fresh mint and fried onions.

Chef's Tips

Always use aged Basmati rice for the best results - the grains stay separate and fluffy. Do not overcook the rice in step 1, as it will continue cooking during the dum process. Using ghee instead of oil gives a richer, more authentic flavor.
